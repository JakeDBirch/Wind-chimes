How to play
Swipe in from the edge
Left or right for the next scale. Each one has its own colour. Custom brings up a keyboard to pick your notes.
Circle around the outside
Register. Clockwise raises the pitch, one octave per turn.
Circle over the chimes
Clockwise adds a chime, anticlockwise takes one away. Three to twelve.
Press and hold
A gust of wind.
Double-tap the clapper
Choose the sound: metal, or something you record with the mic and keep in your bank.
